01 / Architecture

Theme changes colour, not design.

Every component asks for a semantic role. The active family and mode supply that role’s value. Raw palette names remain below the component layer.

Parchment character

  • Warm neutral ramp with very low chroma.
  • Terracotta coral signals action and active state.
  • Dark mode descends toward warm near-black umber.
  • Best for a quiet, editorial, independent product voice.

Alma Mater character

  • UCSF navy anchors ink and every dark surface.
  • Cool gray replaces bone without changing hierarchy.
  • Eggplant lifts to orchid in dark mode.
  • Extended UCSF hues stay inside status, code, and data visualization.

04 / Surfaces

The hierarchy survives every palette.

Canvas, surface, hover, pressed, and inverse steps must remain perceptually ordered. The sidebar stays one calm step apart from the main canvas.

Two-tone sidebar

The sidebar is neither a dark slab nor a duplicate of the canvas. It is a neighboring plane, separated by a hairline and distinguished by hover and active steps.

Flat by default

Cards and rows use surface plus hairline. Only the composer, popover, and modal families receive their named elevation; light shadow tint follows the palette family.

06 / Technical colour

Code, terminal, and data belong to the family.

Technical surfaces retain their semantic richness while matching the active palette’s ground, temperature, and contrast behavior.

Level zero remains visible against the canvas. Higher levels increase monotonically so order survives grayscale.

Syntax

Plain, comment, keyword, string, number, function, type, operator, inserted, and deleted are authored for every combination.

Terminal

Ground, foreground, cursor, selection, and ANSI 16 follow the same family and mode as the surrounding app.

Data visualization

Sequential, categorical, and diverging scales may use the extended family spectrum, with labels and non-colour encodings where meaning matters.

08 / Behavior

Preference is predictable and complete.

Family and mode persist independently. System mode follows the operating-system preference without changing the chosen family.

Selection

  • Parchment is the default family.
  • Alma Mater is a full alternative, not a partial recolour.
  • Light and dark may be chosen directly.
  • System mode resolves to the operating-system preference.

Switching

  • Apply the new family and mode atomically.
  • Update code, terminal, overlays, and embedded artifacts together.
  • Preserve state, focus, scroll position, and open surfaces.
  • Use a brief colour transition unless reduced motion is requested.
