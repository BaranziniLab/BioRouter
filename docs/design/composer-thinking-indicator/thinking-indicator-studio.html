What is actually wrong

Seven findings, each measured in the tree rather than eyeballed. The first two are the complaint as stated. The rest are why it keeps coming back — and the last one is the reason the obvious fix (delete the composer's dot) is wrong.

01

Two identical dots are on screen at the same time

LoadingBioRouter renders above the composer and TurnActivityIndicator renders in the transcript, and their markup for the dot is byte-identical — the same three spans, the same 16px / 10px / 6px stack, the same two keyframes. The match was deliberate (commit 26a23fd7, “Align working status with composer”); what was not intended is how often both are on screen at once.

deriveTrailingActivity suppresses the transcript pill for WaitingForUserInput, LoadingConversation and while prose is streaming — its comment says “Do not double-narrate.” That list misses Thinking, Compacting, and the whole of tool execution, which is most of a turn. During those, both pills breathe at 1.8 s, 8 px out of line. The transcript one carries strictly more (elapsed time, steer text, a 45 s nudge), so the composer copy is the redundant one.

02

They are 8 px apart, and no padding value can fix it

Both sit in the same 760 px centred column. The transcript pill lands 4 px from the column edge — its wrapper adds nothing, so the inset is just the pill's own px-1 (ProgressiveMessageList.tsx:306). The composer pill lands at 12 px: pl-2 on the row (BaseChat.tsx:2219) plus the same px-1.

But pl-2 is not a mistake to delete. 12 px is exactly where the composer's own context row sits (pl-3, ChatInput.tsx:2150), so the pill is correctly aligned — to the composer's grid. The transcript pill is also correctly aligned, to the transcript's. Two indicators anchored to two different grids cannot be brought into line while both exist. One of them has to go. Turn on the alignment grid above: coral is the column edge, red is the composer's inset, green is flush.

03

Three copies of the dot, and three unrelated pulse vocabularies

The same three spans appear verbatim in TurnActivityIndicator.tsx, LoadingBioRouter.tsx and BioRouterSidebar/RecentChats.tsx. Nothing stops the fourth copy, and nothing keeps the three in step.

Worse, the app runs three different pulse languages at once. The breathing dot is a custom ring + glow at 1.8 s. The tool-call status badge is an 8 px semantic-coloured dot on Tailwind's stock animate-pulse — 2 s, opacity only, no scale (ToolCallStatusIndicator.tsx:29). And the message queue inside the composer card runs a third animate-pulse dot (MessageQueue.tsx:160). Three rhythms, none of them harmonised — which is a large part of why the composer's dot reads as “not quite matching” the one by the tool calls. They genuinely do not match; they are not even the same component.

04

1.8 s is off the motion scale entirely

The duration ladder tops out at --dur-slow: 525ms, which is documented in main.css as “the period of an ambient loop that never ends (the spinner)” — the one tier reserved for exactly this. It has zero call sites. The indicator instead hardcodes 1.8s in a Tailwind arbitrary-value class, twice per copy, six times in the app.

05

The status row costs a layout shift every turn

renderWorkingStatus() returns null when idle, so the row appears and disappears with each turn — the composer jumps ~34 px (24 px row + 10 px mb-2.5) the moment you press Send, which is the exact instant your eye is on the composer. Three of the six specimens below cost zero height.

06

The design system already specified two of these, and neither was built

design.md §4.20 fixes a canonical spinner — “1.5 px stroke, currentColor, 700 ms linear rotation, sizes 14/16/20/24”. There is no Spinner.tsx in the repo; 31 call sites use Tailwind's bare animate-spin (1 s) or lucide's Loader2 instead. §4.18 fixes a streaming caret — “2 px × 1em in --text-muted, blinking at 1 s; removed on completion” — which is exactly the gap finding 06 describes, and it does not exist either.

Three canonical periods now disagree on paper before any of them reaches the screen: 700 ms (§4.20), 525 ms (--dur-slow), 1.8 s (what ships). Whatever lands here should pick one and retire the others, or it becomes the fourth.

07

…but the composer indicator cannot simply be deleted

The moment assistant prose starts streaming, the transcript indicator switches itself off — trailingActivity.ts:140, “the visible text IS the feedback” — and there is no blinking caret to replace it (MarkdownContent has no streaming prop at all; this is deliberate). During prose, and during every gap the transcript indicator suppresses itself for, the composer pill is the only thing on screen saying the turn is alive.

So the two indicators are not simply duplicates. The transcript one is intermittent and per-step; the composer one is continuous and turn-scoped. They only look identical because they were drawn identically. The fix is not to remove one — it is to make them stop pretending to be the same thing.

That reframes the brief. The transcript indicator is the good one and should keep the dot: it is where the user is looking, it is per-step, and it carries the timer. The composer needs a signal of a different kind and a different shape class — one that answers “Biorouter has the floor” continuously, that cannot be mistaken for a dot, and that therefore cannot be misaligned against one.

Six directions

A–C keep the status row and change what lives in it. D–E remove the row entirely. F is a modifier that layers onto any of them. Every one leaves the composer box — its radius, padding, fill and hairline — untouched.

A

Live edge

Zero height · zero new elements

The composer already owns a 1 px border that turns --border-accent on focus. While the agent works, that same edge carries a slow travelling arc of the accent. Nothing is added to the layout, so there is nothing to align and nothing to duplicate — the composer itself is the indicator, which is precisely the “the agent has the floor” reading the transcript dot cannot give.

Focus and working stay distinguishable because they are different kinds of signal: focus is a static colour change on the whole edge, working is motion along it. That distinction survives both states being on at once, which they usually are — the composer autofocuses.

B

Hairline sweep

Reuses an existing primitive

The status row becomes a 1 px rule spanning the column, carrying the same indeterminate sweep the app already ships — .br-progress__fill--indeterminate, which already has a documented reduced-motion fallback and already owns role="progressbar". A line is a different shape class from a dot, so the two indicators stop rhyming, and a full-width rule cannot be 8 px out of alignment with anything — it spans the column by definition.

D

Type carries it

Recommended, with A

Delete the dot. The label is already there, it already says the useful thing, and it is already the part the user reads — so let the motion live in the type itself: a slow light sweep across “Biorouter is working on it…”, and nothing else. The redundancy dissolves because the two indicators no longer share a visual atom: the transcript has a dot, the composer has a sentence. There is no dot left to misalign.

This is also the only option that makes the row narrower rather than busier, and the sweep degrades to plain static text under reduced motion with no fallback to design.

What I would ship

A + D together, with F as a follow-up

They fix different halves of the same complaint and neither one does it alone. D removes the duplicated atom — with no dot above the composer there is nothing to be redundant with and nothing to be 8 px out — and it keeps every word the row carries today, while being a net deletion of code. Its one real weakness is peripheral read: if you are not looking at the words, nothing moves.

A is exactly that missing peripheral signal, and it costs zero height, so the composer stops jumping on Send. Together the composer says “Biorouter has the floor” at the edge of vision and “here is what it is doing” in prose, and the transcript dot goes back to being the only dot in the app — the per-step signal, where the user is actually looking.

F afterwards, because it is the only piece that still says something under prefers-reduced-motion, where both A and D flatten to a static frame. That is a real accessibility gap in every option here, and F is the cheapest way to close it.

Extract the dot into one primitive before touching anything visual. Three verbatim copies across TurnActivityIndicator, LoadingBioRouter and RecentChats is what let the two get out of step in the first place; the sidebar keeps the dot, so the primitive is needed either way.

Move 1.8s onto the scale. --dur-slow exists for ambient loops and has no call sites. Whatever ships should be the first, and the arbitrary animate-[…_1.8s_…] values should go with it — they are exactly the newly-written arbitrary utilities that main.css warns can silently fail to reach the stylesheet under BIOROUTER_NO_HMR.

Delete the dot from the composer row (D). Keep pl-2 — it is what aligns the row with the composer's own context chip at 12 px, and once the dot is gone there is no competing round thing for it to be out of line with. The instinct to unpad the row is the wrong fix; removing the duplicate is the right one.

Add the travelling edge (A) as authored CSS in main.css keyed off a data-working attribute on the composer card — not a Tailwind arbitrary value, for the reason the focus-edge comment right above it already gives.

Look at all three families and both modes before calling it. Only the ink and the accent vary — every surface and hairline is now one shared neutral ramp — but the accent is the whole of specimen A: Parchment #b85a32 / #e8895f, Alma Mater #14828c / #60d0da, Roche Limit #ee6c1a in both. A travelling edge that reads as “working” in coral may read as “focused” in teal, and only a real look will say.

Check the reduced-motion frame deliberately. The global reset in main.css nulls duration and clamps iteration count, which parks A's arc at a fixed angle and D's gradient at a fixed offset. Both need an explicit resting appearance, the way .br-progress__fill--indeterminate already declares one.

Specimens are painted from the app's real tokens under their real names, at real sizes, in the app's real Arial-first stack.
