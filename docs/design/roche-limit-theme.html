BioRouter Design · Theme Family Proposal · Awaiting approval

Roche Limit

A third theme family for BioRouter, drawn from JupyterLab: a white page, recessed grey panels, and one bright orange doing all the signalling. It approaches Jupyter as closely as a theme can — and stops exactly before it becomes a clone.

Family id roche-limit Variants light + dark Tokens 59 × 2 Contrast 164/164 AA Nothing shipped yet

The astronomy

The Roche limit is the closest a satellite can orbit a larger body before tidal forces pull it apart. Approach is survivable. Contact is not.

The design reading

We take Jupyter's structure — white page, recessed cells, flat hairlines, quiet chrome — and hold position just outside the point where evocation becomes imitation.

What that rules out

No In [1]: gutter. No Jupyter logo orange. No Material palette. Those are inside the limit, and crossing it is where a theme stops being a design.

Plate VI

Accessibility ledger

npm run lint:check runs scripts/check-contrast.mjs in CI — a new family that fails it fails the build. The palette was verified independently before this document was written, and three values were corrected as a result.

164
assertions · all pass

Every text token against every ground it can land on, every ink-on-fill pair, every non-text boundary at 3:1, and all 20 syntax stops.

Fixed

Light --text-subtle: #6B6963 → #69675F. The original measured 4.47:1 on --background-focus — a fail rounded up to a pass. The corrected value clears 4.61:1 on the darkest ground it can reach.

Fixed

Dark --text-subtle: #98968F → #9C9A93. Same failure mirrored: 4.34:1 on the dark focus surface. Now 4.56:1.

Fixed

Light --accent-bar: #EE6C1A → #D95B08. The bright anchor is 3.09:1 on white but only 2.81:1 on the grey panel — so rails and status dots would have failed WCAG 1.4.11 the moment they sat on a card. The deeper orange clears 3:1 on every light ground (min 3.14). --background-accent stays #EE6C1A, so fills keep the brightness the brief asked for. Dark mode keeps the pure anchor throughout — it clears 5.1:1 even on the muted panel.

Note

Ink on orange is dark, never white. White on #EE6C1A is 2.81:1 — a fail at any size. --text-on-accent is #1F1E1C at 5.39:1. If a white-text orange button is ever required, the fill must be #AE4700 (5.67:1).

Note

The Warm chrome option above is constrained by this. The obvious warm treatment — ramp step 4 #FADBBB as the focus surface — fails at 4.29:1. The toggle uses steps 2 and 3 instead, which pass. Worth knowing that the warm direction has a hard ceiling.

Carried

--border-input at #C9C9C3 is 1.66:1 on white — below the 3:1 non-text threshold. This is inherited parity: Parchment and Alma Mater both do the same, and it matches Jupyter's own #E0E0E0. Fixing it is a theme-wide change, not a Roche Limit change. Flagged, not silently shipped. Q2

Plate VII

Exportability — the honest answer

You asked that these adjustments be settings you can export later. Here is the true state of the codebase, because the answer changes what needs building.

Today

There is no export, for any setting

Theme state lives only in renderer localStorage under three keys: theme, use_system_theme, theme_family. It never reaches the backend and never lands in a file you can copy. config.yaml has no appearance keys; the Electron settings.json has six keys, none of them appearance.

This is already true of Parchment and Alma Mater. Roche Limit doesn't introduce the gap — it makes it visible.

Tier 1 · ships with the theme

Export / import the appearance block

A versioned JSON blob written through the save-dialog IPC that WorkflowsView already uses — biorouter-appearance.json, holding schema, themeFamily, theme, and useSystemTheme.

~1 day. No backend work. Import needs showOpenDialog + readFile on the preload bridge — only the save side is confirmed wired.

Tier 2 · the real fix

Round-trip through config.yaml

Persist appearance under an appearance: key via the existing /config/upsert + /config/read routes. That file is already what users back up and move between machines, so "exportable" becomes free.

Costs a hybrid read path: index.html reads localStorage synchronously to prevent the theme flash, so config becomes the source of truth and localStorage becomes a sync cache. Separate plan.

Plate VIII

Open questions for you

Three of these are live in the controls at the top of this page — toggle them and judge by eye. Three are architectural and need a decision rather than a preview.

Q1 · previewable

How orange should the chrome be?

Jupyter's own answer is barely: orange is the logo and kernel state, everything interactive is blue. Instrument keeps every hover, active and focus surface neutral — orange appears only on rails, fills and dots. Warm tints the sidebar-active and focus surfaces toward the orange ramp.

Recommend Instrument — the Jupyter-faithful reading, and it keeps orange meaning something.

Q4 · previewable

Do tool-call cards get the cell rail?

JupyterLab's 8px collapser is its strongest structural signature. Extending a 2px rail to the active tool-call card is the single most notebook-legible move available.

Recommend On — but it needs a component change, not just tokens, which breaks the "pure re-colouring" contract Alma Mater set. Your call on scope.

Q6 · previewable

Warm-neutral or pure grey?

Warm-neutral holds every grey under OKLCH chroma ~0.006 — imperceptible alone, but it stops the orange reading as hazard signage against a cool ground. Pure grey is slightly more clinical. Both verified AA.

Recommend Warm-neutral. The difference is subtle; the failure mode it prevents is not.

Q3 · decision

Adopt Jupyter's font stack?

Fonts live in @theme inline, described in-file as the single source of truth, and Alma Mater's precedent is explicit: a family is "a pure re-colouring, same layout, same fonts." Jupyter's stack is near-identical to BioRouter's already.

Recommend no. The win is small and the contract is worth more.

Q5 · decision

Should a family be allowed to change radii?

Jupyter reads as technical almost entirely because of its 2px corners. BioRouter's radius scale lives in @theme, outside family scope — so Roche Limit will always be softer than Jupyter. Changing that means moving --radius-* into :root and touching all four existing blocks.

Recommend not now — real architecture change. Worth revisiting if the softness bothers you in Plate I.

Q2 · decision

Fix the input-border contrast globally?

--border-input fails 1.4.11 in all three families. Raising it to #8E8C86 / #706F69 makes forms compliant and visibly heavier — and makes Roche Limit inconsistent with its siblings unless all three change.

Recommend a separate theme-wide fix, not a Roche Limit special case.
