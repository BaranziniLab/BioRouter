The BioRouter wordmark — the horizontal logo, with the same split underline

Same family as the square icon: Bio in UCSF navy, Router in coral. The underline is a short two-tone bar sitting between the o and the R — navy on the Bio side, coral on the Router side — not a rule under the whole word. The dials tune the design ratios; the gap starts at the 2% you picked for the square mark. On a dark app surface the navy would vanish, so the App dark swatch swaps it for UCSF teal #18A3AC. Dials

700

The letterform weight. 800 matches the heavy square mark; 700 reads a touch lighter for a horizontal wordmark.

0.00em

Tightens or opens the whole word. Measured in em, so it holds at any size.

2%

Space from the word to the underline, as a share of cap height — so it scales with the logo. Starts at the 2% you chose for the square mark.

15%

As a share of cap height. 15% matches the square mark’s bar.

100%

How far the bar spans around the o–R seam. 100% underlines exactly the oR pair; lower tightens it toward the seam, higher reaches further under each word.

150 px

Preview only — the wordmark is vector and scales freely. Not part of the exported design.
