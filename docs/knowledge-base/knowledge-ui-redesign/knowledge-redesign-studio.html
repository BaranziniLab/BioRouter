Design study · Knowledge section · Rev 2 — 2026-08-22

One subject, one canvas, two rails.

The Knowledge section names its knowledge base twice, fences the canvas between two 36-pixel control strips, floats its inspector over the very thing it describes, and solves its node colours to text-contrast ratios so they come out nearly black. It also responds to the window when the thing that changes size is the pane — which is why it breaks first at BioRouter's own minimum window. This is the proposal to fix all of it.

Every size

All four frames below are the same markup, rendered into containers of four different widths and laid out entirely by @container queries. Nothing is drawn twice, and nothing is a wireframe — if a step works here it works because the CSS made it work.

The bug this table exposes. minWidth: 1000 is derived in main.ts as 240 px of sidebar plus the 760 px reading column — so at the app's own minimum with the sidebar open, the Knowledge pane is 760 px. But the section's one breakpoint, md: at 930 px, is a viewport media query. The viewport is 1000 px, so it fires and lays out two columns; the 300 px Sources rail then leaves 444 px for a graph pane whose filter strip needs about 681. The section is at its most broken at the smallest size the app will let you make. Every step below is therefore a container query on the pane, not a media query on the window.
02

The shell

Three bands become two. The base is identified once, in a 48-pixel subject bar whose name is the switcher — so the page header carries the title and nothing clickable at all. Below 620 px of pane height the header sheds its description line, which is the difference between a usable and an unusable canvas at a 600-pixel window.

On the brief's premise. The selector never rendered above the banner — it is the second child of the header's title row, co-linear with the h1, and the built bundle agrees with the source. The real defect it points at is worse than misplacement: the base was identified twice, 40 px apart. Deleting the header cluster and promoting the subject band to switcher removes the duplicate rather than relocating it.
03

The filter bar

The filters read as buttons because they are buttons — the same component and variant as “Manage bases”, sharing the input's exact 8-pixel radius, in a 36-pixel strip with zero vertical padding. A selected filter never changes its own fill. And there is no answer at all for what happens when they stop fitting.

A filter is a boxed control, not a button and not a pill

36 px, no padding, 8 px radius on bothBefore
Filter by name or type Type Predicate Source Status
  • 2 px of air above and below a 32 px input
  • Input and filters share the same 8 px radius
  • Selected state is an appended badge only; the fill never changes
  • No overflow behaviour. They just stop fitting.
48 px bar, element radius, 1.5 px edge, solid when onAfter
Filter by name or type Type 2 Predicate Source
  • --radius-element (8 px) — the same step as every button, input, select and tab. A filter is not differentiated by being a different shape from the rest of the app
  • 1.5 px edge on a transparent ground, against the app's 1 px hairlines: it reads as a field that holds a value, where a secondary button is a filled slab with no edge at all
  • Engaged is a solid accent fill, not a tint — visible at a glance across a 1600 px bar
  • A real 13 px stroked caret, not a 9 px text glyph

When the row stops fitting

Three states, chosen by pane width, in a fixed priority order. Nothing ever wraps to a second line — a filter bar that grows steals canvas — and nothing ever scrolls sideways, which is the failure the legend already demonstrates.

What this page can and cannot prove. The mockup's filter row is about 60 px narrower than the shipped component, so it clears the old 1060 threshold that the real row overflowed by 44 px. The 810 px and 1110 px figures therefore describe the shipped row, measured there; this page illustrates the ladder they produced, and is not independent evidence for them.
The priority order is a decision, not an accident. Search collapses last because it is the only control that can reach a node whose type the user does not yet know. Type keeps the one always-visible slot because it is the facet every base has — Predicate and Source are empty on a legacy base, and Status only ever holds four values — and because it is the facet the legend beside it is about. Predicate is the first of the rest to go for a reason that is pure arithmetic: at 105 px it was the widest chip in the row, so surrendering it bought the most width per facet given up. What folds into More carries its own count, so nothing is hidden silently: a filter you cannot see is still reported by the number on the control that swallowed it.

One rule covers all seven. A surface that returns a value to the page is a popover anchored to the control that opened it; a surface that is a task is a modal with a scrim; a surface that describes the current selection is the rail, or the rail's fallback at narrower panes. The inspectors are the only ones that break the rule today, and they break it in the direction that costs a focus trap.
05

The canvas

Every node is a circle. The twenty Biomedical Entity types are drawn solid, the eight Provenance & Context types hollow, and a referenced-but-unwritten node keeps BioOKF's dashed ring — so a channel that survives monochrome still exists, without a second silhouette anywhere. Labels are always on and haloed, which is what makes an all-circle canvas readable.

Proposed marks — solid entity, hollow provenance, dashed external After
The trade, stated plainly. The seven silhouettes carried node family because cross-family colour distance under simulated dichromacy bottoms out at ΔE00 0.00. They are gone, and the opt-in preference that would have restored them was withdrawn rather than built. What carries the redundancy instead is the graph's keyboard model (spec §5.12), which is built: the canvas is one tab stop with arrow-key traversal and an aria-live region that speaks Multiple sclerosis, Disease, Clinical — identifier, type, family — on every focus change. That is a wider channel, not a consolation. A silhouette is a visual redundancy: it served a sighted viewer with dichromacy and nobody else, and only while the mark was large enough to resolve. A spoken label serves a blind reader and a screen-reader user too, and it does not degrade with mark size. The residual cost is real and stays stated: cross-family distance still bottoms out at ΔE00 0.00, so a viewer with tritanopia sees Phenotype and Food as one colour and one shape, told apart only by the label, hover, the legend and the inspector — and all four are deliberate acts, where a silhouette was passive. One structural channel does survive monochrome: the twenty Biomedical Entity types are solid discs, the eight Provenance & Context types hollow rings at 1.7 px, and an external node keeps its dashed ring.
06

The palette

The fills read dark for an identifiable reason: they are solved to hit WCAG contrast ratios against the ground — rungs of 3.5, 4.5, 5.8 and 7.3, and a Provenance ladder running to 12.0. Those are text rungs applied to a mark. BioOKF instead puts a near-black hairline around every circle and lets the fill be pretty. The fix is two numbers.

The step between adjacent members stays at 0.055 L against today's 0.050–0.059, so within-family separation survives by construction — measured with a real CIEDE2000 across all seven families, the minimum lands at 4.80 against a guard floor of 3.0. The ground does not move: check-contrast.mjs asserts a six-scope --background-muted identity, and the single shared graph palette exists precisely because that identity holds.

The legend

Today's legend is a 36-pixel strip that scrolls sideways, and its two states show disjoint information: collapsed is inert — no buttons at all — and shows 28 unlabelled swatches; expanded is interactive but drops the evidence key entirely, and its collapse control sits at the far end of whichever scroller is active. It appears in §01 as a rail at 1626 px, a card at 1040, and a chip below that.

What the swatch must carry is the fill and the solid/hollow distinction, and a square carries both at 10 px more legibly than a disc does.
08

The sources rail

Rebuilt in real Chrome against the repo's own stylesheet at the true 300-pixel width: the resting empty state is 899 px tall in a 745 px viewport at the app's default window. It scrolls by 154 px before the user has done anything at all.

BlockHeightWhat it is, and what happens to it
