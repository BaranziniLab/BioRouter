Protocol

  1. Thaw competent cells on ice.
    Move a 50 µL aliquot of competent E. coli from −80 °C onto wet ice; let it thaw ~10 min. Never warm by hand — heat kills competence.
  2. Add plasmid DNA and rest on ice.
    Pipette 1–5 µL (1–10 ng) plasmid into the cells. Flick gently to mix; do not vortex. Incubate on ice 30 min.
  3. Heat-shock at 42 °C.
    Transfer the tube to a 42 °C water bath for exactly 45 s, then return it to ice for 2 min. Timing is critical.
  4. Recover in SOC at 37 °C.
    Add 950 µL room-temperature SOC. Shake at 37 °C, 200 rpm, for 60 min to let resistance express.
  5. Plate on selective agar.
    Spread 50–100 µL onto a pre-warmed LB plate with the correct antibiotic. Incubate at 37 °C overnight (12–16 h).
  6. Count and screen colonies.
    Count colonies to gauge efficiency, then pick well-isolated colonies for colony PCR or miniprep.
